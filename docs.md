Architectural choice for this project

The orchestrator agent controls the workflow for the 2 features in ChainGuardAI(Onboard suppliers and monitor them)

First feature Agent Pipeline

Find Supplier Agent: Receives the query from he user via asi:one and finds a supplier best suited for their business
Financial Agent: Evaluates the financial risk between the country where the business owner is located and the location of the supplier
Compliance Agent: Evaluates the ethics and sustainability values of the supplier


Second Feature Agent Pipeline

Logistics Agent: Showcases and evaluates the quality and shipping tracking of the supplier

Demand Agent: SHowcases and evaluates the demand of the products
